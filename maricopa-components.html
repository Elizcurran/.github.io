Maricopa County Component Library

USWDS 3.x components themed to the County brand. Each element is shown with the contrast ratio that governs it — the library doubles as accessibility evidence.

This is a visual reference for the Americaneagle build, not production code. Production components must be generated from themed USWDS 3.x Sass, so keyboard and screen-reader behavior comes from GSA-maintained JavaScript. Colors, type, and state rules here match the token specification exactly.

Buttons

All button labels are set in Poppins Bold as the house style. The primary-button decision is the one that most often breaks: white on Sunrise Orange fails at 3.12:1 — so the default primary is Summer Night Blue, and the orange variant carries a Night Black label.

Links & body text

Sky Blue is the only link-safe brand color at 5.23:1. Links keep their underline; color alone never distinguishes them (WCAG 1.4.1).

Residents can renew a permit online, check a case status, or find a service near them. Body copy is Night Black at 17.02:1, well above the 4.5:1 floor, and stays readable at every zoom level the county supports.

On Monsoon Grey surfaces darker than the 25% tint, links switch to Summer Night Blue — Sky Blue drops to 4.74:1 on grey and shouldn't be pushed further.

Alerts & site messages

State colors are USWDS defaults, deliberately not rebranded. Sunshine Yellow can't carry warning text (2.05:1), and orange has no danger semantics. Every alert pairs an icon with text so meaning never rests on color alone.

Tabs

A custom pattern — USWDS has no core tabs, so this one is Maricopa-maintained. Active tab is marked by a Sunrise Orange underline (non-text, 3.12:1) plus a color and weight shift, so state never rests on color alone. Arrow keys move between tabs; Home and End jump to the ends.

Apply for building permits, schedule inspections, and track application status through the online portal.
Search parcel records, view ownership history, and look up assessed property values by address or parcel number.
Register to vote, confirm your registration, find your polling place, and view certified election results.

Because tabs hide content by default, don't put anything a resident must see — deadlines, legal notices, contact info — behind a tab that isn't the default-open one. That's the trap USWDS avoided by leaving tabs out.

Focus indicators

The fix for the earlier trap: one two-color ring instead of a context-dependent single color. An inner Sunshine Yellow ring plus an outer Summer Night Blue ring means at least one always contrasts — no matter what tile color an editor drops the button onto. A transparent outline sits underneath so focus survives Windows High Contrast Mode.

On white — navy ring carries · 15.81:1
On navy — yellow ring carries · 7.72:1
On orange — navy ring carries · 5.08:1

Same ring in all three cases. Nothing about the button changes with its background — which is what makes it safe in Full Site Editing, where the surface behind a component isn't known in advance.

In-page navigation

An "on this page" jump list that tracks the current section as you scroll. The active item is marked by an orange left border and aria-current, so it's conveyed beyond colour.

Overview

The residential building permit program covers additions, remodels, and new structures within the county's jurisdiction. Most applications are handled entirely online.

Eligibility

Property owners and licensed contractors may apply. Work in incorporated cities is handled by that city rather than the county.

How to apply

Create or sign in to your resident account, start a permit application, and upload your site plan and supporting documents for review.

Scroll the page and the sticky list highlights the section in view. Each link needs a real heading target — which makes the Anchor links pattern below a dependency, not a nicety.

Process list

Numbered, explanatory steps for a linear task — an ordered list, so screen readers announce the sequence. Not for form progress; use the step indicator below for that.

  1. Check your eligibility

    Confirm the property is in the county's jurisdiction and gather the parcel number.

  2. Gather your documents

    Prepare a site plan, project scope, and contractor details if applicable.

  3. Submit your application

    Complete the online form and upload your documents for review.

  4. Track your status

    Follow review progress and respond to requests from My Services.

Built on <ol> with CSS counters — the numbers are real list semantics, not decorative text, so the order survives with styles off.

Step indicator

Progress through a multi-step form: completed (orange), current (navy), upcoming (grey). The "Step 2 of 4" text and aria-current="step" carry the state; the bars are reinforcement, not the only signal.

Step 2 of 4 — Applicant information

  1. Start (completed)
  2. Applicant information
  3. Documents
  4. Review & submit

Completed steps read "(completed)" to assistive tech via visually-hidden text; the current step is marked aria-current="step". Colour alone never signals progress.

Modal dialog

A true dialog: focus moves in, is trapped while open, and returns to the trigger on close. Escape and the backdrop both dismiss it.

Marked role="dialog", aria-modal="true", and labelled by its title. In production the page behind should be set inert so nothing outside the dialog is reachable — the one piece a demo can't fully show.

Once submitted, your permit application is sent to Planning & Development for review. You can track its status any time from My Services.

Auto-advance is intentionally omitted: a moving carousel must offer a visible pause and stop on hover and focus (WCAG 2.2.2), and most civic carousels are better off manual. Inactive slides use hidden, so they leave the tab order and the screen-reader flow.
